Apple Developer Program ($99/yr)

Our immediate community goal: funding the annual Apple Developer membership to code-sign and notarize releases — eliminating Gatekeeper warnings and sudo xattr terminal workarounds for everyone.

Zero Paywalls & No Telemetry

Commercial cleaners charge $40/year subscriptions and run invasive background daemons. MacOSCleaner has zero locked features, zero analytics, zero network calls during cleanup, and will never monetize your data.

Engineered in Pure Swift 6

Built exclusively for Apple Silicon (M1–M4) with Swift 6 structured concurrency and actors. High-speed multi-core scanning across 1,887 cleanup definitions and 275+ apps, running in <30 MB RAM with zero Electron bloat.

Forensic Depth & 10 Languages

A 5-level deep uninstaller with 30 evidence types, continuous rule updates for developer tools (Docker, Xcode, AI CLIs, uv, Cargo), and complete parity across 10 global languages.
